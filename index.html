Vision

To empower unemployed youth and individuals across India by equipping them with inclusive, practical, and future-ready skills that enable them to become job creators rather than job seekers. Through quality training, entrepreneurship development, and innovation-driven programs, we aim to build a skilled workforce that contributes to sustainable economic growth. We are committed to promoting financial independence for women by ensuring equal opportunities in learning, leadership, and enterprise development. By supporting local manufacturing and encouraging indigenous industries in line with the Make in India initiative, we strive to strengthen self-reliant communities. Ultimately, our goal is to contribute to the vision of Aatmanirbhar Bharat by fostering a culture of skill, innovation, and entrepreneurship that enhances the nation’s cultural strength and economic prosperity.

About Us

Research Education Society (RES) is a leading educational and skill development organization dedicated to empowering individuals through quality education and industry-oriented training. With a strong commitment to academic excellence and social responsibility, RES has been at the forefront of transforming lives and communities since its inception.

Empowering Minds, Transforming Lives Since 1990. For over three decades, the Research Education Society (RES), Bidar, has been more than just an institution, it has been a medium for change. Established in 1990, we have dedicated ourselves to a singular mission i.e, bridging the gap between potential and opportunity. From our humble beginnings in Bidar, RES has evolved into a premier multi-dimensional network. We serve as a vital link between academic excellence, vocational mastery, and social welfare, ensuring that every student, youth, and marginalized individual we reach has a pathway to a sustainable future.
