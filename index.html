About Us

Research Education Society (RES) is a leading educational and skill development organization dedicated to empowering individuals through quality education and industry-oriented training. With a strong commitment to academic excellence and social responsibility, RES has been at the forefront of transforming lives and communities since its inception.

Empowering Minds, Transforming Lives Since 1990. For over three decades, the Research Education Society (RES), Bidar, has been more than just an institution—it has been a catalyst for change. Established in 1990, we have dedicated ourselves to a singular mission: bridging the gap between potential and opportunity. From our humble beginnings in Bidar, RES has evolved into a premier multi-dimensional network. We serve as a vital link between academic excellence, vocational mastery, and social welfare, ensuring that every student, youth, and marginalized individual we reach has a pathway to a sustainable future.
