Computer Training Program

Programme Details

The Computer Training Programme offered by Research Education Society focuses on building practical digital skills for students and job seekers. Participants learn computer fundamentals, office applications, internet usage, and modern digital tools that are essential for today's workplace.

The program combines theoretical learning with hands-on practice to ensure students gain real experience. After completion, students are prepared for employment opportunities and higher digital literacy.
